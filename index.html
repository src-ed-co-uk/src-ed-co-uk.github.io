My name is Stephanie

Throughout my twenty four year teaching career, I have taught in both mainstream and special schools, working with learners who have Specific Learning Difficulties (including dyslexia, dyspraxia, dysgraphia), Autism, ADHD, Profound and Multiple Learning Difficulties, and Severe Learning Difficulties.

Why have an independent assessment?

Schools can screen for dyslexia and put support in place, but waiting lists are long and many do not offer a full diagnostic assessment. An independent assessment gives you a clear picture of your child's — or your own — strengths and difficulties within weeks, together with a written report and specific recommendations that you can share with school, college, university or an employer.

Because the report is written by an APC-holding assessor, it can be used as evidence for exam access arrangements and for Disabled Students' Allowance (DSA) applications, and it stays valid for life.

More about me

As an individual who was diagnosed with dyslexia at 41 years old and coming from an incredibly neuro-diverse family, I fully appreciate how learning differences can significantly impact an individual’s experience of their education and how it can affect their emotional and mental well-being while in education and during their working and home life.

School is not the only place an individual can get access to support. Through my role as an assessor for dyslexia and as a SpLD teacher, my working philosophy is to aim to engage both children and adults, helping unlock their potential and ensuring they understand any learning differences they may have.

It’s important to learn how to live with them, use them to their advantage, and learn strategies to help them succeed and progress in their learning and career journey.

I hold an Assessors Practicing Certificate (APC); this has to be renewed every three years. During this period it is expected by the awarding body that I complete up to 30 hours of CPD (5 hours SASC approved) each year.

My memberships of The Dyslexia Guild ensure I am able to stay abreast of changes in practices and standards.
